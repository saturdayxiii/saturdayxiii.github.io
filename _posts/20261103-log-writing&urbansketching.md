## Creative Log
-  **Writing:** I learned last story that I can overcome some writers block by... writing. Like talking it out with chat-gpt: this is what I'm trying to do, this is where I'm stuck, these are things I can try. And that seems to work well as a warm up to actually getting through my problems. And it matters very little what gpt responds with, I just like it for it's encouragement mostly. So this time around I'm trying to do my own responses as well... if I need it. I have a notes page asking direct questions: do I want char1 to be like this? what happens if? how can these stakes be raised here? and it's been going fine enough without me pretending to dialogue or role play sycophantism.
