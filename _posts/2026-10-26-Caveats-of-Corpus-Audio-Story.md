Audio version of Caveats of Corpus has gone public today. My current magnum opus, but hopefully that will change soon as I improve.

Really love the cover image of this one.  I keep getting comfortable making scenes for the visuals, but when I made this one I was less comfortable with Blender and needed to simpler.  I need to visit that mindset more.

Sometimes Evan includes them in his narration, but I did the sfx to this one. Evan does great narration, but I was gratefully surprised how much the sfx elevated this particular story. They weren't even that great to work with, it just all came together in the end.
